# M8.4.14 - Pixel pyramids of the multi-gigabyte fixtures (Windows, development evidence)

Raw data: [compression](M8.4.14-compression-windows.json), [all fixtures](M8.4.14-pyramids-windows.json), [cancellation](M8.4.14-cancellation-windows.json),
[rgb2g with the final code](M8.4.14-rgb2g-final-windows.json). Tool: `tools/measure_tile_store.py` (the engine's coordinator in the tool's process, the build in
its real method worker; memory is the process tree sampled every 25 ms by `tools/process_tree_sampler.py`). Computer: Windows 11, AMD Ryzen 7 7700X
(16 logical CPUs), 31 GiB, Python 3.12.4, NumPy 1.26.4, libvips 8.18.7, four threads (the engine's cap). Fixtures of [M8.4.13](M8.4.13-large-baseline-summary.md),
read from `C:\sedcore-large-fixtures`, pyramids written to `C:\sedcore-pyramids` and deleted after each file. The disk cache was warm (the files had
been written or read shortly before); cold-cache numbers are not claimed. One run per file.

## Compression (step 4 of the card, rule stated before measuring)

| `rgb2g` (2 GiB) | Build | Throughput of source | Disk | Tile read p95 (read, inflate, verify) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| no compressor | 20.4 s | 100.6 MiB/s | 2.68 GiB | 4.9 ms |
| zlib level 1 | 51.8 s | 39.6 MiB/s | 1.64 GiB | 8.5 ms |

Rule: zlib if its throughput is at least 70 % of the uncompressed one **and** its tile-read p95 is at most 10 ms. Ratio 0.39: **no compressor** for format
version 1 (contract §8.2). zlib would save 39 % of disk at 2.5 times the build time.

| `jpeg` 4,096 x 65,500 | 0.75 GiB | 6.9 s | 7* | 1.01 GiB | 241 MiB | 0.18 s | 20 of 20 (level 0) |

*MiB/s of the 47 MiB JPEG file, not of its decoded size. Exactness: 20 regions per level, levels 0-3, crossing tile seams, compared byte for byte with the
generator's pixels (level 0) and with an independent NumPy form of the rounded integer mean applied to those pixels (levels 1-3). The JPEG is lossy, so only
its level 0 is compared, with libvips' own decoding: it checks the tiling, not the decoder. Every first progress event arrived within 0.30 s.

Targets of the card: memory well below the 512 MiB working budget of contract §8 (and the 2 GiB whole-program target of §7, which also counts Electron);
progress at least once a second (0.80 s at most, the striped file, whose strips are read in larger blocks). The disk is about 1.33 times the decoded image.

## Cancellation (target: acknowledged within 250 ms)

The first measurement **failed**: 297 ms on `rgb2g` cancelled at 30 %, and a repeated test grew with the progress, from 43 ms at 5 % to 658 ms at 95 %,
because the worker deleted the partial folder (gigabytes of written tiles) before answering. Fixed in the card: cancellation is checked before every tile, and the
partial folder is deleted in the background after the answer (it keeps its `.partial-` name, so a start or the next build removes anything left). After the fix,
twelve cancellations (ten points of level 0 from 5 % to 95 %, two of the hash stage): **median 11.8 ms, maximum 14.6 ms**; every partial folder was gone
within 0.61 s; nothing was left behind. `rgb2g` measured again with the final code: 17.5 s, 117 MiB/s, exact, cancellation 12.6 ms. The other six files were
measured with the code before this fix, which differs only in the cancellation checks and the background deletion.

## Tile reads through the coordinator (fresh tile cache, warm disk cache)

One tile: p50 5.2-6.2 ms, p95 8.2-18.2 ms. Sixteen tiles in one request: p50 81-108 ms, p95 98-140 ms, with one p95 of 403 ms (`gray16_2g`, one slow batch of
ten). These numbers stop at the coordinator; card M8.4.15 adds the main process and the page, and its rule (16 tiles p95 <= 150 ms, one tile p95 <= 40 ms)
decides whether tiles go through the engine or are read by the main process.

## Not measured

macOS and Linux timing (the GitHub runs check correctness only), a cold disk cache, a hard disk instead of an SSD, a 16 GiB computer, several builds at once
(the engine runs one job at a time), and repeated runs of the six files other than `rgb2g`.
